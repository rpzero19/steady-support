Steady

A simple, private habit tracker for iPhone.

Common questions

How do streaks work? A streak counts the scheduled days in a row that you completed. Days a habit is not scheduled never break it, and today only counts against you once the day is over.

Can I fill in a day I forgot? Yes. Open History, tap the day, and check the habit off.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Steady. The medium widget lets you check habits off without opening the app.

Reminders are not arriving. Check that the habit has "Remind me" switched on, and that notifications are allowed for Steady in the iOS Settings app.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Steady has no account and no servers. See the privacy policy.
