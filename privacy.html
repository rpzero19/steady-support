Steady Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version

Steady does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Steady stores

The habits you create, their schedules and reminder times, and the days you mark them done. This is saved only in the app's private storage on your iPhone, and is shared with the Steady home screen widget on the same device so it can show today's habits.

What Steady does not do

There is no account and no sign-in. Steady has no servers, contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, Steady schedules a local notification on your device. Reminders are never sent through a server, and you can turn them off per habit or in iOS Settings.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your Steady data may be included in that backup under Apple's terms. Steady itself never receives it.

Deleting your data

Delete a habit to remove it and its history. Deleting the app removes everything Steady has stored.

Children

Steady collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. For questions, use the support link on Steady's App Store page.
